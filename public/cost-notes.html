In US dollars, at 1,400 won per dollarabout $380

Parts the team already owned (ESP32, camera, speaker, microphone) are not counted.

Comparison price

The EMILY robotic lifeguard sells for about $10,000 in the United States (Military.com, 2016). $10,000 ÷ $380 is about 26, so Delphylax costs about 1/26 as much to build. EMILY is a finished product that reaches 25 mph, so this compares price, not ability.
